# V3 CONTRACT — binds every step of the Map-v3 build
The audit contract (CONTRACT_AUDIT.md) still governs proof. THIS contract governs SCOPE — it exists because the known failure mode is silent narrowing.

1. **Full breadth is the default.** Every zone, every direction, every approach-event type, every coin, full history, weekends in. Any dimension not yet covered is listed as REMAINING — never silently absent.
2. **Measurements before assumptions.** No fixed constants (0.6U-style) may define geometry or thresholds unless they were first measured in this study or are banked certifications, and the source is named inline. Substituting an assumption for a measurement must be flagged the moment it happens.
3. **Reading ≠ money.** V3 is a reading study: mining is unrestricted, full history always, no sealed split. The seal is owed only when a geometry built on v3 claims P&L — at that moment it leaves v3 and gets its own prereg.
4. **No verdict words on readings.** "Killed / works / edge / validated" are forbidden inside v3. Readings are frequencies with n, and forecasts are scores. Only a future money test may use verdict words.
5. **Conditionals are first-class.** Every number ships as full-history headline PLUS its splits (regime, day-type, weekday/weekend, coin, zone-strength, origin, intensity). Instability across splits is promoted as a regime marker, never averaged away or discarded.
6. **n beside every number. UNDERPOWERED (n<40) is re-queued, never concluded.** FDR correction applies only at certification time, not during exploration.
7. **Forecasts must be scored.** Any "X% chance of reaching zone N" claim carries a Brier/skill score against the naive base rate on held-out days, or it is labeled UNSCORED.
8. **The scope ledger is mandatory.** Every build step ends with: EXAMINED (this step) · REMAINING (from the design) · DROPPED/DEFERRED (with reason). A missing ledger is itself a violation.
9. **Deviations from MAPV3_DESIGN.md are reported in the same message they occur.** Never promoted, never silent.
10. **The check phrase is binding.** When Svet pastes the V3 CHECK paragraph, the full ledger + violations (if any) are produced before anything else proceeds.
