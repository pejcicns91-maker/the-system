# C3 — MULTI-TOUCH: CRACK OR HARDEN? (2026-07-08)
*Mode MAP · all 457,856 zone-entry episodes on 64,291 touched level-days, full history, weekends in · paired within same day-zone (selection-proof) · regime columns · files: touches_v2 (already shipped). (b) line: episodes = the recorder's mechanical entry runs; sided entries for direction stats (open-inside 4.1%, excluded there); pairing conditions on days having ≥k episodes — no thresholds invented.*

## MULTI-TOUCH IS THE NORM
Of touched level-days: only **12.1% are one-and-done**; 59.5% have **5+ separate entries**. Zones get knocked all day.

## THE PAIRED ANSWER: NEITHER CRACK NOR HARDEN, PER KNOCK
Same day, same zone: P(pass through) episode 1 → 2: **31.7% → 32.8%** (+1.1pp, n=38,764 pairs); episode 1 → 3: +0.2pp (n=33,595). Penetration does not deepen (median 0.03 → 0.02U — episodes are mostly edge-grazes); dwell stable. Era (E1 +3.0 / E2 +1.8), weekday/weekend, and all four assets agree the delta is ~0–3pp. **The "third knock breaks the door" folklore has no support at episode grain.**

## THE REAL SIGNAL RUNS THE OTHER WAY (day grain, retrospective label — knock count is known only as it accumulates)
P(the day finally resolves THROUGH the zone at its last episode), by total knocks: 1 knock **36.9%** · 2: 36.8 · 3: 36.3 · 4: 34.0 · **5+: 30.1%** (n=6.7k…38k). Monotone down ~7pp. **A day that keeps knocking is a rejection day persisting, not a break loading** — each additional test mildly *lowers* the through-odds. Live use: accumulating knock count = drifting fade-lean, the opposite of the folklore.
