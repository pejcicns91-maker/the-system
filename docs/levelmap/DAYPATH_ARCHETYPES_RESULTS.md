## THE LEVEL FINGERPRINTS (what price does at which level, per archetype)
Three facts hold across EVERY archetype, and one is direction-conditional:
1. **The POC group gets touched 65–79% of days regardless of day shape** — the magnet is unconditional; no day type escapes it.
2. **Overnight edges 55–67% everywhere; weekly/monthly levels only join big days** (16% on churn → 28–33% on trends).
3. **First PD-edge contact is mid-morning on every archetype** (median 9–11 ET) — the board engages early no matter what the day becomes.
4. **The day's direction decides WHICH levels give way, overwhelmingly**: on up-archetypes, upside daily edges break 52–99% (vs 43% base) while downside edges break 0–14%; mirror on down-archetypes. On churn, both sides hold (8–32%).

## HONESTY BOX — what this is and is not
An archetype label is known **only when the day is over**. These fingerprints are the day's anatomy, not a morning forecast. Their live uses are exactly two, both real: (a) **partial-path matching** — at 11:00, match today's path-so-far to the archetype library and inherit the remainder's statistics (the named next step, not yet built); (b) **yesterday's archetype as a dial** — already certified in D (after-trend/after-lean effects). FA fingerprints partly restate the cluster's own definition (big up day → upside breaks); the non-tautological content is in the invariants (1–3), the timing, and the FC shape splits.
