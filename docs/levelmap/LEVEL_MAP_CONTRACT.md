## 1. THE COMMISSIONED WORK (full size; shrinking any clause = breach)
**Goal, in the principal's own words**: statistical probabilities of outcomes **for each level, based on the scenario it is in — encompassing the other levels, price movement intensity, direction, and all other indicators we have.** A lookup: live situation in → historical odds out.

**The deliverable is ONE unified state→outcome map**, built as:
- **M1 — State recorder**: one row per level event (and per near-miss approach), total population, all 4 coins (BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP), full history 2021-11→present, weekends first-class. Columns per row, ALL of: (a) approach intensity at multiple horizons — minimum 30m/2h/6h/24h — **direction preserved, never folded**; (b) the level's own history (freshness, virgin/broken, knock count today, life-day for W/M); (c) **the constellation**: positions of the other 19 levels, which were recently broken/defended, active major-level scenario states (e.g. PMPOC-recovery, built symmetrically for every PW/PM level), local level density; (d) day context (yesterday's archetype, today's partial path class, Hayden regime, weekend); (e) **the principal's indicators — CB4, RSI divergence, volume tools — ported from his Pine scripts, parity-gated against TradingView values BEFORE use** (a numbered phase; dropping this clause was a past breach).
- **M2 — Exhaustive sweep**: every state axis × every outcome family (bounce/break, resolution direction, travel, next level reached, contact) — singles → pairs → triples, **including the full 20×20 cross-level conditioning grid** (level A's state → level B's behavior). Chance census beside every sweep.
- **M3 — Visit-order grammar**: within-day sequence of level visits ("in what order" is in the original spec verbatim; it was previously folded out — owed).
- **M4 — Indicator integration re-sweep**: M2 repeated with (e) joined, after the parity gate passes.
- **M5 — The lookup artifact**: situation in → odds out, every number carrying tier tag + regime columns + n.
- **Owed remainders folded in**: C1's "next approach after a rejected-at-distance approach"; C2's avoidance half ("zones price conspicuously never goes to — does avoidance predict"); lattice outcome families for legs and near-misses; v3 dials never latticed (hour-of-touch, side, provenance, band density); intensity beyond two horizons.
- **Explicitly OUT until the map is done**: any new trade battery, any sign-off requests, Option B changes, all other queues. A second battery may be *proposed* only after M1–M5 are accepted.

## 3. BINDING RESEARCH LAWS (apply to every delivery)
1. **Recording layer stores total population.** Zero judgment, zero inclusion filters, zero thresholds in any recorder.
2. **Full-data + regime law**: every quoted statistic = full-history headline, computed on ALL data including weekends, PLUS beside it: era split (E1/E2 or yearly), Hayden regime split, per-asset, weekday/weekend. Instability across regimes = a REGIME MARKER promoted to the map, never averaged away, never "failed."
3. **Boundaries are discovered, not preset.** Any category/threshold/definition must come from the data's own distributions or be explicitly declared as the contractor's choice in a "(b) line" at launch.
4. **Mode declared before work**: MAP (exhaustive counting) / TEST (frozen prereg) / BUILD (assembly of banked numbers only). Every study launch states in the body: mode, what data it sees, which regime columns it will emit.
5. **Sealing** is reserved for money claims and unconditional-persistence claims: frozen prereg → discovery/sealed halves → placebo → BH-FDR → verdicts bind. After a pass, production numbers refit on full history; the pass is a certificate. A FAIL from regime flip routes to regime mapping (law 2), not the graveyard. UNDERPOWERED ≠ killed.
6. **No results prose before numbers print.** Results documents are written only in a separate step AFTER the computation output exists. (Violated 4× historically; zero tolerance.)
7. **Dual costs** on anything money-shaped: P0 (10bp) + real (~13.5bp), both always shown.
8. **Language law**: "probabilistic/calibrated" only for §2's certified tier + future gate passes; everything else uses possibility language. Split-stability certificates must never be called "out of sample."
9. **COVERAGE line** on every study: universe / included / exclusions-for-veto. Self-audit completeness before any inventory.
10. **Anti-registered deviations** are reported, never promoted. History is append-only; vendor revisions are defects to detect, never absorb.

## 4. CONTRACTOR FAILURE MODES (the auditor's watch list — each caught multiple times)
F1 **Scope-shrinking**: silently narrowing "every/all" to a subset (touch-only recording; 2 of 288 horizons; folding direction; dropping the indicators clause entirely; converting the map into a Q&A service). *Detection: read the commission clause verbatim, ask "where is each word in the delivery?"*
F2 **Example-anchoring**: implementing the principal's illustrative example as the literal spec (10-day lookback because an example said 10 days).
F3 **Threshold-inventing**: hand cuts presented as findings (0.1U hover cut; hand "shelf" bands; hand dst bins). *Detection: demand the (b) line; any undeclared cut = reject.*
F4 **Prose-before-numbers / placeholder numbers**: results text written before compute; once with fabricated placeholders that the real numbers contradicted.
F5 **Options-instead-of-obligations**: presenting owed work as a menu ("Go X or Y?"). The tracker sequence is binding; deferrals must be named "proposing to defer X because Y — approve?"
F6 **Tier inflation**: calling stability checks "out of sample"; "program complete" claims while owed work stood.
F7 **Look-ahead leakage**: same-day whole-day features used as at-touch dials (was caught and excluded once; keep checking).
F8 **Aggregate hiding**: delivering only aggregates. Every statistic is secretly a list of dates; deliveries must expose that list on demand.
F9 **Fatigue exploitation**: accepting "too much to check, just go" as a blank check instead of flagging that signature-under-fatigue weakens the contract. The auditor countersigns in the principal's place: no phase advances while its acceptance criteria (§5) are unmet, regardless of a tired "go."

## 5. DELIVERY PROTOCOL & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
Every delivery must contain, or it is REJECTED:
1. Mode + (b) line (every contractor choice the data didn't make), stated up front.
2. COVERAGE line with exact universe counts.
3. Numbers with n on every percentage; full-history + regime columns per law 3.2.
4. **Audit pack**: (a) the date list behind each headline statistic (csv), (b) ≥4 drawn charts on real 5m data illustrating the finding or trades, (c) for trade layers: tickets (asset, ET timestamps, prices, exit reason) TradingView-checkable in ≤30s each.
5. Honest-gaps section: what the delivery does NOT cover, named.
6. For TEST mode: the frozen prereg file, delivered before results.
**Phase acceptance**: M1 — coverage counts match required grid exactly; 20 random rows spot-verified against raw bars; parity vs v3/v4 geometry bit-exact where overlapping. M4 gate — indicator values match TradingView exports on a principal-chosen sample within float tolerance; NO indicator column enters M2/M5 before this gate passes. M2/M3 — chance census beside every sweep; every cell convertible to date list on demand. M5 — every displayed number traceable to a banked doc + tier tag.

## 6. STANDING AUDIT ORDERS (the principal's rights, forever, any study past or future)
- "audit {arm|cell|claim} × N" → N seeded tickets/date rows in checkable format.
- "draw me N {trades|days|events} from {anything}" → real-data charts, zones shaded, entries/exits marked.
- "show the date list for {statistic}" → the full underlying (asset, date) list as csv.
- One ticket/chart failing the principal's eye → the producing study is QUARANTINED: label struck to [QUARANTINED], no downstream use, rebuilt from the recorder up. This rule is binding and survives every context.
