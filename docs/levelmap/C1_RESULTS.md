## THE CURVES vs PLACEBO
**Occupancy**: price spends **2.2× more time parked just outside real zone edges than placebo predicts** (27.5 vs 12.4 per-mille at the edge), with the placebo's own occupancy peak sitting ~1U away from its fake zones — i.e. right where the *real* zones are. Zones organize where price parks; the structure detector works from either frame.
**Push-or-bounce (per-bar drift)**: real ≈ placebo everywhere (differences ≤0.7pp around 48–49%). **Honest null: no per-bar pull/repel force exists.** A level's action is visible in where price *dwells* and in which *path family* the day takes — not in the next bar's direction.
