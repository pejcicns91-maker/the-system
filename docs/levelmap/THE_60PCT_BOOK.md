## THE THREE HONEST BOUNDARIES (what ≥60% does NOT exist for — proven, not assumed)
1. **Direction before the approach forms.** Which way the day resolves ≈ coin flip until you can see how price arrives. The 60% cells above are all *conditional on the arrival being visible*.
2. **Mechanical money.** Every cell above was inside the F battery's space: 186,208 trades, 0/100 arms survived costs+seal. These are reading odds at YOUR entries, not autopilot odds.
3. **Unlimited stacking.** Each added condition divides n. The table stops where the data stops (thin cells flagged); a 90% cell with n=12 is noise wearing a suit and is not in this book.
